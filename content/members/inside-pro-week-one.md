<p>Welcome in. The room is the product. The calls and the shelf are what you come back for, but the thing that changes your week is having a place to say "here is the situation" and get a straight answer the same day.</p>
<h2>Three habits</h2>
<p><strong>Post the objective on Monday.</strong> One line: the number you are chasing this week and the one thing in the way. The moderators and the room work from that, not from a vague "how do I get better."</p>
<p><strong>Post the win on Friday.</strong> Wins are how the room learns what works. A win with the detail of how it happened is worth more than a screenshot.</p>
<p><strong>Download one pack and run its build prompt before the call.</strong> Show up to the live call with a system you already built, not a question about whether to build one.</p>
<h2>For the first call</h2>
<p>Bring the real situation, with the real numbers. The call is not a lecture. It is Aton working through what the room brings, in order of who is closest to a decision.</p>
