# ONBOARDING (about 30 minutes)
1. The business, the offer, the value of a conversion, the primary metric, and the number that keeps a campaign alive.
2. The account: monthly spend, campaign types running, who clicks the buttons, how often reviews happen.
3. Rules: daily cap per move, minimum spend before KILL, the search terms waste line (percent of spend on zero-conversion terms), days before SCALE.
4. Exports: campaigns and ad groups (all time or 24 months, daily) with cost, conversions, cost per conversion, conversion value, clicks, impressions, CTR, search impression share and lost to budget; plus the search terms report for the last 90 days. Save to /data.
5. Install audit: compute percentiles into /memory/BENCHMARKS.md, label every campaign and ad group, produce the first negatives list into /memory/NEGATIVES.md, and seed the winners and losers.
6. Deliver the first audit, the negatives to add today with the waste they stop, and three moves. Wait for the green light.
