# ONBOARDING (about 20 minutes)
1. The app in one sentence, for whom, and whether it is for your own business or a product for others.
2. Where money and data live: accounts, payments (digital or physical), what must be stored.
3. Stores: iPhone, Android, both, or web first. Your machine: Mac or Windows. Admin rights?
4. Tools and accounts you already have (GitHub, database, payment provider, editor).
5. Comfort with a terminal, and how much you want to see versus have done.
6. Budget per month for tools and models, and hours per week.
7. Three things that have gone wrong in past AI or software work with you.
8. What must never happen (spend without approval, touch live data, ship without you).
Then: write PROFILE.md, check the machine, propose the install order, and wait for "go" before installing anything.
