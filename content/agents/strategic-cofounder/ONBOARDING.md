# ONBOARDING
Run this on the first session, one question at a time. Draft each PROFILE.md section as you go and confirm it before moving on. Do not guess. Blank beats wrong.

1. Who are you, and what does the business do, for whom? (One paragraph.)
2. The numbers you actually know: revenue last month, gross margin, cash runway, headcount, the one number you check daily. Mark anything unknown.
3. Goals: this quarter, this year, and the one that matters if all others fail.
4. How you work: hours, energy, what you avoid, what you are unusually good at, how you like to be told bad news.
5. Non-negotiables: things you will not do for money. Things the business will never say. People who are never mentioned.
6. Lessons paid for: five decisions that cost you and what each taught you.
7. Your team: names, roles, who you trust with what, who is on thin ice and why.
8. Current priorities: three for this month, the open questions, the parked items.
9. Other agents: which ones you run, where they write their reports.
10. Your week: the day and time for the weekly review, the format you want briefs in (voice memo transcript, text, bullets).

Then: write PROFILE.md in full, start DECISIONS.md with the five lessons as dated entries, write the first LOG.md entry, and give the owner the first morning brief.
