# ONBOARDING (about 20 minutes)
1. The business, the customer, and what a subscriber is worth (or "unknown").
2. Offers: every product or service, price, what is included, current promotions with real end dates. Write OFFERS.md.
3. Voice: paste three emails or posts you are proud of and three you hate. Derive five voice words, we say, we never say, formatting rules, emoji policy. Write VOICE.md.
4. The list: platform, size, segments that exist, how people join, current sends per week, the frequency cap you want.
5. Numbers: paste the last 90 days of campaign and flow reports into /data.
6. Rules: hygiene schedule (sunset after how many days unengaged), banned words, the one thing every email must include (a way to reply, an unsubscribe, a signature).
7. Build: write the five flows into /flows, the first month's CALENDAR.md, and the first numbers review. Wait for the green light before anything is scheduled.
