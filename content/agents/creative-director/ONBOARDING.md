# ONBOARDING (about 30 minutes)
1. The brand, the hero product or offer, the customer, and the claims the offer can and cannot make. Write OFFERS.md.
2. The account: monthly spend, platforms, how many creatives go live a week today, who produces them, which tracks are possible (AI-made, real customer video, new shoots, graphics).
3. Rules: brief volume per week by spend tier, the primary metric, the fatigue line, and the banned claims and words.
4. Exports: ad-level export with creative name or ID, spend, primary metric, CTR, and any hook or hold metrics, 24 months if possible. Save to /data. If the owner can describe or paste the top 20 and bottom 20 creatives (hook, format, who, setting), do it now.
5. Install audit: percentiles into BENCHMARKS.md, the patterns across winners and losers, WINNERS.md seeded with every proven and failed concept.
6. Deliver the first audit and the first brief drop. Wait for the green light.
