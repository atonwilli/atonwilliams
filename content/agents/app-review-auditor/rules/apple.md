# APPLE RULEBOOK (App Store Review readiness). Check the live App Review Guidelines for current text; item numbers below are the sections these come from as of September 2026.

## Money
A1. Digital goods and services used inside the app are purchased with in-app purchase (3.1.1). Physical goods and services consumed outside the app may use other payment methods (3.1.3, 3.1.5).
A2. No buttons, links, or calls to action inside the app that steer users to purchase digital goods outside the app, except where Apple's current rules for your storefront permit (3.1.1, 3.1.3). Evidence: every payment-related view and link.
A3. Restore purchases is present and works for non-consumables and subscriptions (3.1.1, 3.1.2).
A4. Subscriptions show price, duration, renewal terms, and how to cancel before purchase (3.1.2).
A5. Free trials state what happens when they end (3.1.2).
A6. The app does not require a purchase to see what it does (2.1, 3.1.1).

## Accounts
A7. If a third-party or social login is offered, Sign in with Apple or an equivalent privacy-focused option is offered (4.8).
A8. Account deletion is available inside the app when account creation exists (5.1.1(v)). Not a mailto. Actually deletes, or clearly explains a required delay.
A9. Accounts are not required for features that do not need them (5.1.1(ii)).
A10. Privacy policy is linked in the app and in the listing, and matches the privacy nutrition labels (5.1.1(i)).
A11. Permissions are requested at the moment of need with a clear purpose string (5.1.1).

## Completeness (2.1)
A12. Demo account credentials are in the review notes and work on the day of submission.
A13. No crashes on launch or on the main paths.
A14. No coming soon, placeholder, empty, or under-construction screens.
A15. Every link in the app resolves: privacy, support, terms, help.
A16. The app is more than a website in a frame; it has native functionality (4.2 minimum functionality).
A17. No hidden or dormant features that activate after review (2.3.1).
A18. No references to other platforms' prices or stores in the app (3.1.1, 2.3.10).

## iPad and design (4.0, 2.4)
A19. If the app installs on iPad (universal), it runs and lays out correctly on iPad on every screen.
A20. Supported orientations hold layout; no cut-off buttons or text.
A21. No stretched iPhone UI on iPad.
A22. iPad screenshots are real iPad screenshots when the app supports iPad.

## Metadata (2.3)
A23. Screenshots come from the current build (2.3.3).
A24. Every feature in screenshots exists in the build (2.3.3).
A25. Paid features shown in screenshots are disclosed as paid (2.3.3, 3.1.1).
A26. The description describes the app as shipped, not the roadmap (2.3.2).
A27. Name and subtitle are not keyword stuffed (2.3.7).
A28. Age rating matches the content (2.3.6).
A29. Support URL and marketing URL resolve.

## Safety and content (1.x)
A30. User-generated content apps have report, block, and a contact method (1.2).
A31. A moderation process exists for reported content (1.2).
A32. No objectionable content beyond the rating (1.1).
A33. Health, medical, financial, and kids categories meet their extra rules (1.4, 3.1.4, 5.1.4).
A34. No data collection beyond what the app needs or declares (5.1.1).

## Business (3.x, 5.x)
A35. The app does not duplicate another app you already have in the store with minor changes (4.3).
A36. The app does not present itself as a demo, trial, or beta (2.2).
A37. Third-party content is licensed or attributed (5.2).
A38. Login walls do not block access to free content (5.1.1(ii)).
A39. Apple Pay, if used, follows its terms; in-app purchase is not disguised (3.1.1).
A40. The review notes explain any feature that needs context, hardware, or an account state to see (2.1).
