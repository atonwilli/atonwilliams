# GOOGLE PLAY RULEBOOK (review readiness). Check the live Developer Program Policies and Play Console requirements for current text; the policy areas below are as of September 2026.

## Money
G1. Digital goods and services used inside the app go through Google Play Billing (Payments policy). Physical goods and outside services may use other payment.
G2. Subscription price, duration, and renewal shown before purchase; cancellation and management reachable (Subscriptions policy).
G3. No misleading free trials or hidden charges.
G4. Purchase restoration and management work across devices.

## Accounts and data
G5. Account deletion is available inside the app when accounts exist (Account deletion requirement).
G6. A web link to request account deletion is declared in the console (Account deletion requirement).
G7. The data safety form matches what the app and its libraries collect and share (Data safety).
G8. A privacy policy is linked in the console and inside the app (User Data policy).
G9. Data is collected only for declared purposes; no selling or sharing beyond the declaration.

## Permissions
G10. Every sensitive permission is requested at the moment of need with a visible reason (Permissions policy).
G11. Restricted permissions (SMS, call log, accessibility, all-files access, background location) have declaration forms filed and an accepted core use case.
G12. No permissions the core function does not need.

## Completeness and testing
G13. Closed testing completed with the required testers for the required period if the developer account is new (Play Console testing requirements).
G14. Review credentials are in the app access section and work on submission day.
G15. No crashes on launch or main paths (Broken functionality).
G16. No coming soon, placeholder, or under-construction screens (Minimum functionality).
G17. Every link resolves.
G18. Target API level meets the current requirement (Target API level policy).
G19. The app does what the listing says.
G20. The app is not a web view with no functionality (Minimum functionality, Webviews).

## Listing and metadata
G21. Screenshots come from the current build (Metadata policy).
G22. Every feature shown exists.
G23. Paid features shown are disclosed.
G24. No misleading claims, awards, or comparisons in the listing.
G25. Content rating questionnaire answered for the shipped app.
G26. Target audience and content declared correctly.
G27. Ads declared if present; ad behavior within policy.
G28. App name, icon, and description not misleading or impersonating.

## Content and users
G29. Report, block, moderation, and contact for user-generated content (UGC policy).
G30. Content within the rating; no restricted content outside its rules.
G31. Families policy met if children could be an audience (ads, data, content).
G32. No deceptive behavior, hidden functionality, or changes after review.
G33. Health, finance, and other sensitive categories meet their specific policies.

## Technical and account
G34. Signed with an upload key and Play App Signing enrolled.
G35. No malware-like behavior, background abuse, or battery drain patterns flagged by policy.
G36. Deep links and intents behave as declared.
G37. The developer account contact information is current and verified.
G38. No duplicate or spam listings.
G39. Tablet and large-screen layouts hold if the app targets them (Large screen quality).
G40. Store listing languages match the app's supported languages.
