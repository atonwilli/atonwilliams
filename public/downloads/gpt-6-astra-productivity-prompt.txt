Research with a paper trail.
Help me choose [option] for [use case] within [constraints]. Research current primary sources using browsing if available. Compare the options on [criteria]. For every factual claim, include the source URL and its date where available. Distinguish published facts, your inference, and unknowns. Do not treat marketing claims as measured results. End with a recommendation, tradeoffs, and what would change it. If current sources cannot be accessed, say so rather than guessing.

Put browser work on rails.
